1. Information We Collect

To provide you with the best property discovery experience, we collect information when you interact with our platform.

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and authentication credentials.
  • Contact Details: Phone numbers for host-tenant coordination.
  • Property Data: For hosts, we collect listing details, verification documents, and location data.
  • Usage Information: Details of how you use our platform, including search history and property views.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to enhance your experience and ensure a secure marketplace.

  • To facilitate seamless communication between tenants and hosts.
  • To process payments and unlock precise property locations securely.
  • To perform identity verification and maintain platform integrity.
  • To provide personalized property recommendations based on your preferences.

3. Information Sharing

Transparency is our priority. We only share information under the following circumstances:

We share necessary contact details with hosts once you initiate a booking or location unlock request. We also partner with verified service providers like M-Pesa for payment processing.

4. Data Protection

HomeView employs industry-standard security measures including SSL encryption and secure cloud storage to protect your data from unauthorized access or disclosure.

5. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You can manage these settings directly through your profile dashboard or by contacting our support team.
